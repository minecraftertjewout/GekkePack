# GekkePack

**GekkePack** is a modpack focused around [Create](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create) and its many addons.

The pack also includes popular technology and automation mods such as:

- **CC: Tweaked**
- **Mekanism**
- **Create addons**
- And many other technology-focused mods

## About

GekkePack is made for players who love **Create**, automation, engineering, and technology mods.

Whether you enjoy building complex factories, automating processes, or experimenting with powerful technology, GekkePack has something for you.

> ⚙️ **If you love Create and technology mods, GekkePack is for you!**
