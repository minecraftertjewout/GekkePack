# GekkePack

GekkePack is a Minecraft modpack focused around Create and its many addons.

The pack also includes popular technology and automation mods such as:

- Create and its addons
- CC: Tweaked
- Mekanism
- And many other technology mods

## About

GekkePack is made for players who love Create, automation, engineering, and technology mods.

Build complex factories, automate processes, and experiment with a wide variety of technology-focused mods.

If you love Create and technology mods, GekkePack is for you!

## Features

- Create-focused gameplay
- Lots of Create addons
- Technology and automation
- CC: Tweaked
- Mekanism
- Many other supporting mods

## Modpack

GekkePack is designed to combine Create's mechanical automation with powerful technology mods, giving you plenty of ways to build, automate, and expand your world.
